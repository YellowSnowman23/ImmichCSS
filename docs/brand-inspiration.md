# Palette inspiration

Claude Theme and GPT Theme are unofficial community interpretations for Immich Dark appearance. They are not official themes or exact replicas of the products. The colors below were observed on official websites on October 31, 2026; website styling is not necessarily a published brand-wide color specification.

| Theme | Source | Observed colors | Adaptation in this repository |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Theme | [Claude homepage](https://claude.com/) and [Claude product](https://claude.ai/) | Terracotta `#D97757`, warm ivory `#FAF9F5`, near-black text `#141413` on the homepage | Retains terracotta at primary-400 and ivory as text. Lifts the main accent to `#E18C6D` so Immich's filled button hover state remains readable. Warm charcoal surfaces `#191817` / `#292724` and the remaining ramps are authored adaptations. |
| GPT Theme | [OpenAI Design Guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) | Black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF`, and supporting cool gray `#C9D1D9` in the observed page styling | Interprets the monochrome identity with charcoal `#18191B` / `#282A2D`, white text, and an off-white `#E8EDF2` accent. `#C9D1D9` appears in the neutral ramp; the rest of the ramps are authored adaptations. |

The GPT palette follows OpenAI's black-and-white identity rather than asserting that a historical green is its current official primary color. The unauthenticated ChatGPT page was also inspected, but its light appearance and sign-in controls do not establish the palette of the authenticated dark interface.

The themes preserve Immich's own typeface and layout. OpenAI Sans, Anthropic typefaces, logos, wordmarks, and other company assets are not included. Names identify the inspiration only; there is no affiliation or endorsement.
